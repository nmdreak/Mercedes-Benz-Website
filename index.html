Our Heritage

A History of Innovation

Mercedes-Benz traces its origins to the pioneering automotive work of Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler. Their contributions helped shape the modern automobile.

Today, the brand continues to combine engineering, luxury and innovation in its vehicles.

Sporty by Design

Mercedes-Benz Coupes

Mercedes-Benz coupes are known for sleek styling, sporty proportions and performance-oriented design. Models have included the CLE Coupe and Mercedes-AMG GT.
